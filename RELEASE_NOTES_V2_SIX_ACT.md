# The Cost of Courtesy — Story-First V2 Six-Act Release

**Author:** Christopher Hayes, CDAIO  
**Research approach:** Secondary synthesis of published evidence; no original study or joint politeness × language × energy experiment  
**Date:** November 11, 2026

## What changed

The Story-First Edition is now a complete six-act narrative rather than three redesigned excerpts. The new chapters are:

- **Act 3 — The cheapest answer isn't always the best one.** An explicitly hypothetical task-level comparison helps the reader distinguish a short initial response from a verified outcome. No fabricated energy or performance numbers.
- **Act 5 — Where does the work go?** A conceptual infrastructure view shows that a user's location does not identify a serving facility. It compares a 0.245 Wh mean thank-you reply in a specific Llama/H100 experiment with a 0.24 Wh median Gemini Apps production prompt under a distinct Google-scale methodology. The latter's 0.26 mL water estimate remains attached only to its study.
- **Act 6 — The words stayed the same.** One unchanged human message, "Thank you.", becomes three possible product designs: a generated response, a lightweight acknowledgment or quiet closure. Trade-offs are presented as **untested hypotheses**, not ranked energy savings.

Existing Acts 1, 2 and 4 retain their original visuals, source-aligned measurements and interactive controls, including the full 122-language source cohort and five-model courtesy comparison. A mobile chapter wayfinder links the entire six-act narrative. Optional evidence disclosures explain the measurement boundary or hypothetical status of each new exhibit.

## What did not change
