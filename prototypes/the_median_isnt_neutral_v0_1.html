01. Mean and median use the entire cohort

All 122 canonical rows enter both reference statistics. The highlighted twelve are an editorial subset, never the reference population. An interquartile band marks the central 50%.

02. Cost and accuracy remain separate

Measured per-output-token energy and benchmark accuracy have different units. The views deliberately avoid an arbitrary “efficiency–equity index.” Strict accuracy can be very sensitive to formatting.

03. Visual prominence is not causal inference

Language-resource tiers follow the source data. Colour and flags are descriptive; language, model serving, tokenization, scripts, outputs, and evaluation protocols can all matter. This does not isolate the causal effect of language or politeness.
